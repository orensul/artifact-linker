The Gutenberg Poetry Corpus is a dataset compiled by Allison Parrish, containing over 3 million lines of poetry extracted from hundreds of books available on Project Gutenberg. Each line of poetry in the dataset is associated with a unique 'gutenberg_id', with a total of 1,191 distinct IDs, indicating the source book from which the line was extracted. The dataset is monolingual, focusing solely on English poetry, and is categorized under the size range of 1 million to 10 million entries. It is designed for use in text generation and language modeling tasks, making it a valuable resource for researchers and developers working in the fields of poetry analysis, stylistics, and natural language processing. The dataset is available under the CC0 license, allowing for unrestricted use. The corpus is particularly useful for those interested in exploring poetic structures, generating new poetic content, or training language models on poetic text. The accompanying GitHub repository provides additional resources, including usage examples and instructions for creating customized versions of the corpus, further enhancing its utility for various creative and analytical projects.
