The Inspec dataset is a collection of abstracts from 2,000 English scientific papers, primarily in the domains of Computers and Control and Information Technology, published between 1998 and 2002. This dataset is designed for benchmarking keyphrase extraction and generation techniques. Each abstract is annotated with two sets of keyphrases: controlled and uncontrolled. Controlled keyphrases are derived from the Inspec thesaurus and may not appear in the text, while uncontrolled keyphrases are selected by professional indexers after reviewing the full articles, with a higher likelihood of appearing in the text. The dataset is divided into training, validation, and test splits, containing 1,000, 500, and 500 documents respectively. Keyphrases are categorized into extractive (present in the text) and abstractive (not present in the text) types. The dataset includes BIO tags for sequence labeling tasks, facilitating the use of contextual language models for keyphrase extraction. This dataset is particularly valuable for researchers focusing on keyphrase extraction and generation, providing a unique resource with professionally indexed keyphrases, unlike many other datasets that rely on author-assigned keyphrases. The dataset is available for download and use in various natural language processing tasks.
