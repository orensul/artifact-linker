The Harvard USPTO Patent Dataset (HUPD) is a comprehensive collection of English-language utility patent applications submitted to the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) from January 2004 to December 2018. The dataset is structured to support various tasks such as fill-mask, summarization, text classification, and token classification. Each patent application is stored in a distinct JSON file, identified by its application number, and includes 34 data fields. These fields cover a wide range of information, including application and publication numbers, title, decision status, filing and publication dates, classification codes, inventor and examiner details, and sections like abstract, claims, background, summary, and full description. The dataset is designed for use in tasks like patent acceptance prediction, automated subject classification, language modeling, and abstractive summarization. It is particularly useful for research in machine learning and natural language processing, focusing on intellectual property. The dataset is publicly available under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International license, and aims to facilitate research that could lower the barriers to patent application drafting, potentially broadening access to intellectual property protection.
