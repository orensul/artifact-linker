The WCEP10 dataset is designed for summarization tasks, specifically focusing on multi-document summarization. It is derived from the Wikipedia Current Events Portal and is compatible with the Transformers library's summarization script. The dataset is monolingual, containing English text, and falls within the size category of 1,000 to 10,000 instances. It is structured to support conditional text generation tasks, including summarization and text-to-text generation. The dataset offers four configuration options for document concatenation: 'roberta', 'newline', 'bert', and 'list', each providing different methods of combining document texts. The data fields include 'id' for paper identification, 'document' for the body of text, and 'summary' for the abstract. The dataset is divided into three splits: train, validation, and test, with 8,158, 1,020, and 1,022 instances respectively. This dataset is intended for use in developing and evaluating summarization models, providing a robust resource for researchers and developers in the field of natural language processing. It is particularly useful for those working on summarization algorithms that require handling multiple documents to generate concise summaries.
