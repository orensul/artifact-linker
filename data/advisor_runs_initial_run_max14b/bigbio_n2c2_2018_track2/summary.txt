The n2c2 2018 ADE dataset is part of the National NLP Clinical Challenges, focusing on the extraction of medication information and adverse drug events (ADEs) from clinical narratives. This dataset is monolingual, in English, and is not publicly available. It is designed for tasks such as Named Entity Recognition (NER) and Relation Extraction (RE). The dataset is particularly aimed at identifying and classifying concepts related to medications, their signature information, and ADEs, which include allergic reactions, drug interactions, overdoses, and medication errors. These ADEs are significant as they account for a substantial portion of hospital adverse events, yet they are preventable. The dataset supports three main tasks: Concept Extraction, Relation Classification, and End-to-End processing of raw narrative text to discover and relate concepts to medications. The intended use cases involve improving health delivery by identifying potential drug interactions and alerting caregivers to prevent ADEs. This dataset is part of a broader effort to enhance natural language processing in clinical settings, providing a platform for researchers to develop and compare systems for extracting critical health information from unstructured clinical text.
