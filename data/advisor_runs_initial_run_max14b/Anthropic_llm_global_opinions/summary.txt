The GlobalOpinionQA dataset is a collection of survey questions focused on global issues and opinions, adapted from the World Values Survey and Pew Global Attitudes Survey. It is designed to analyze the opinions that large language models (LLMs) reflect on complex global issues, aiming to uncover potential biases in AI systems. The dataset is available in a CSV format, containing columns such as 'question', which holds the text of the survey question, 'selections', a dictionary mapping country names to lists of respondent percentages for each answer option, 'options', a list of possible answers for each question, and 'source', indicating whether the question originates from the Global Attitudes Survey or World Values Survey. The dataset falls within the size category of 1K to 10K entries and is primarily in English. It is intended for use in evaluating the representation of subjective global opinions in language models, although the creators acknowledge limitations in its construct validity for this purpose. The dataset can be accessed and loaded using the 'datasets' library in Python, specifically with the identifier 'Anthropic/llm_global_opinions'.
