The VitaminC dataset is a comprehensive resource designed for fact verification tasks, specifically focusing on text classification within the domain of natural language processing. It is based on Wikipedia edits and revisions, providing a robust framework for evaluating and improving fact-checking models. The dataset is monolingual, containing English language data, and is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 license. It falls within the size category of 100,000 to 1 million entries, making it a substantial dataset for research and development purposes. VitaminC is particularly notable for its contrastive nature, where claim-evidence pairs are nearly identical except for subtle factual differences. This design challenges models to discern and adapt to slight changes in evidence, enhancing their robustness. The dataset is intended for use in tasks such as fact-checking and natural language inference, providing a valuable benchmark for developing models that can handle adversarial scenarios. Researchers and developers can utilize VitaminC to train models that are sensitive to factual nuances, ultimately improving the accuracy and reliability of automated fact verification systems.
