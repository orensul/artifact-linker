The Consumer Finance Complaints dataset is a comprehensive collection of consumer complaints related to financial products and services. It is designed to provide insights into issues consumers face in the financial marketplace. The dataset is monolingual, containing English language entries, and is categorized under text classification tasks, specifically topic classification. It includes over one million entries, making it a large dataset suitable for various analytical purposes. The dataset is structured with multiple features, including 'Date Received', 'Product', 'Sub Product', 'Issue', 'Sub Issue', 'Complaint Text', 'Company Public Response', 'Company', 'State', 'Zip Code', 'Tags', 'Consumer Consent Provided', 'Submitted via', 'Date Sent To Company', 'Company Response To Consumer', 'Timely Response', 'Consumer Disputed', and 'Complaint ID'. Each entry provides detailed information about the complaint, such as the type of financial product involved, the specific issue, and the company's response. The dataset is intended for use in regulatory analysis, consumer education, and empowerment, as well as for research in consumer finance. It is available under the CC0 1.0 license, allowing for unrestricted use. The dataset is updated daily, ensuring that it reflects the most current consumer experiences and trends in the financial sector.
