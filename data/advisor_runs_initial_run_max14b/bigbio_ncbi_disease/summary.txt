The NCBI Disease dataset is a comprehensive resource designed for the biomedical natural language processing community, focusing on disease name recognition and concept normalization. It is a monolingual dataset in English, licensed under CC0 1.0, making it freely available for public use. The dataset is fully annotated at both the mention and concept levels, providing detailed information for tasks such as Named Entity Recognition (NER) and Named Entity Disambiguation (NED). The dataset is accessible through its homepage on the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) website. It is linked to PubMed, ensuring that it is a credible and authoritative source for researchers. The dataset is intended to support various research activities in the domain of biomedical informatics, particularly those involving the identification and normalization of disease names in text. By offering a structured and annotated corpus, it aids in the development and evaluation of algorithms and models aimed at improving the accuracy and efficiency of disease-related information extraction from biomedical literature.
