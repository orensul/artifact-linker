The DDI Corpus is a specialized dataset designed for tasks in the biomedical domain, specifically focusing on named entity recognition (NER) and relation extraction (RE). It is a monolingual English dataset that has been manually annotated to include information on drugs and their pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic interactions. The corpus comprises 1025 documents sourced from the DrugBank database and MedLine, making it a valuable resource for researchers and developers working on drug interaction studies and information extraction in the biomedical field. The dataset is publicly accessible and is hosted on GitHub, with a license under CC BY-NC 4.0, allowing for non-commercial use. The DDI Corpus is particularly useful for developing and testing algorithms that aim to identify and extract drug-related entities and their interactions from text, providing a robust foundation for advancements in biomedical informatics. Its inclusion in PubMed further underscores its relevance and utility in academic and clinical research settings. The dataset's structured annotations facilitate the training of machine learning models aimed at improving the accuracy and efficiency of drug interaction detection systems.
