Taskmaster-1 is a goal-oriented conversational dataset designed to facilitate research in dialogue modeling and text generation. It comprises 13,215 task-based dialogues across six domains, created using two distinct methods: the 'Wizard of Oz' (WOz) approach and 'self-dialog'. In the WOz method, trained agents and crowdsourced workers interact to complete tasks, while in self-dialog, workers script entire dialogues themselves. The dataset is monolingual, featuring English language dialogues, and is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License. It is divided into two configurations: 'one_person_dialogs' and 'woz_dialogs'. The 'one_person_dialogs' configuration includes train, validation, and test splits with 6,168, 770, and 770 examples respectively, while 'woz_dialogs' contains 5,507 training examples without additional splits. Each dialogue instance includes a conversation ID, instruction ID, and a series of utterances, each with an index, speaker role (USER or ASSISTANT), text, and optional semantic annotations. The dataset is intended for use in developing and evaluating dialogue systems, particularly in tasks related to text generation and fill-mask applications.
