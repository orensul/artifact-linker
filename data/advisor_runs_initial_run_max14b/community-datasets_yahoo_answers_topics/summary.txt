The YahooAnswersTopics dataset is a large-scale text classification dataset derived from the Yahoo Answers platform. It is designed for topic classification tasks and contains over 1 million entries, making it suitable for training and evaluating machine learning models in the domain of text classification. The dataset is monolingual, with all entries in English, and is sourced from the extended Yahoo Answers corpus. It includes several features such as 'id', 'topic', 'question_title', 'question_content', and 'best_answer'. The 'topic' feature is a class label with ten categories, including Society & Culture, Science & Mathematics, Health, Education & Reference, Computers & Internet, Sports, Business & Finance, Entertainment & Music, Family & Relationships, and Politics & Government. The dataset is split into a training set with 1,400,000 examples and a test set with 60,000 examples. The total download size is approximately 533 MB, and the dataset size is around 793 MB. This dataset is particularly useful for researchers and developers working on text classification models, providing a rich source of real-world data for training and testing purposes.
