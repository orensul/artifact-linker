The GovReport-QS dataset is an extension of the GovReport dataset, specifically designed for summarization tasks. It includes annotated question-summary hierarchies for government reports, which are intended to enhance document structure comprehension and content engagement. The dataset is monolingual, containing English language data, and is categorized within the size range of 10,000 to 100,000 entries. It is available under the CC BY 4.0 license. The dataset is structured into two configurations: 'paragraph' and 'document'. The 'paragraph' configuration provides paragraph-level annotated data, while the 'document' configuration aggregates paragraph-level annotations for entire documents. Each data instance includes a document ID, document sections with titles and paragraphs, and question-summary pairs that highlight the hierarchical structure of the content. The dataset is split into training, validation, and test sets for both configurations, with the 'paragraph' configuration having 17,519 training instances, 974 validation instances, and 973 test instances. The 'document' configuration includes 1,371 training instances, 171 validation instances, and 172 test instances. The dataset is curated by experts and sourced from editors of the Congressional Research Service and U.S. Government Accountability Office, ensuring high-quality annotations for research and development in summarization tasks.
