The ClimateCommitmentsActions dataset is a monolingual English dataset designed for text classification tasks, specifically focusing on identifying climate-related paragraphs about commitments and actions in corporate disclosures. It contains between 1,000 and 10,000 entries, with a total dataset size of 666,342 bytes. The dataset is split into a training set with 1,000 examples and a test set with 320 examples. Each entry consists of a 'text' field, which is a paragraph extracted from corporate annual and sustainability reports, and a 'label' field, which is a binary classification indicating whether the paragraph discusses climate commitments and actions ('1') or not ('0'). The dataset was created by experts and students from Universität Zürich and Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg, and it is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International license. The primary use case for this dataset is to support research and development in the field of climate-related financial disclosures, enabling the identification and analysis of corporate commitments to climate action. The dataset is available for non-commercial use, and further details can be found on the ClimateBERT website.
