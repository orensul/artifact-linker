The 'financial-news-articles' dataset is a collection of financial news articles, designed to support research and development in the field of natural language processing and financial analysis. The dataset comprises three main features: 'title', 'text', and 'url', all of which are stored as strings. This dataset is structured into a single split named 'train', which contains 306,242 examples and occupies approximately 848 MB of storage space. The total download size of the dataset is around 492 MB. The primary domain of this dataset is financial news, making it particularly useful for tasks such as sentiment analysis, topic modeling, and information retrieval within the financial sector. Researchers and developers can leverage this dataset to build and evaluate models that require a comprehensive understanding of financial news content. The dataset is accessible via a Kaggle link, providing a valuable resource for those interested in exploring the intersection of finance and machine learning. It is an ideal choice for projects aiming to enhance automated systems in financial news analysis and related applications.
