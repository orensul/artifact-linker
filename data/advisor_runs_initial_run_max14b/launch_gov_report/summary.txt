The GovReport dataset is a collection of government reports and their corresponding summaries, created by expert language creators. It is designed for the task of summarization, specifically focusing on long document summarization. The dataset is monolingual, containing English language documents, and is licensed under CC BY 4.0. It includes reports from government research agencies such as the Congressional Research Service and the U.S. Government Accountability Office. The dataset is categorized in the size range of 10,000 to 100,000 entries. It offers three configurations: 'plain_text', 'plain_text_with_recommendations', and 'structure', each providing different formats of the data. The 'plain_text' configuration is the default, used for text-to-text summarization, while 'plain_text_with_recommendations' includes additional recommendations in the summaries. The 'structure' configuration provides data with section structures. The dataset is split into training, validation, and test sets with 17,519, 974, and 973 instances respectively. The dataset aims to facilitate research in summarizing lengthy documents by providing comprehensive context and longer summaries compared to other datasets in the domain.
