The DailyDialog dataset is a high-quality, multi-turn dialogue dataset designed to reflect daily communication patterns. It is monolingual, containing dialogues in English, and is categorized under text classification tasks, specifically multi-label classification. The dataset is expert-generated and includes annotations for communication intention and emotion. It comprises between 10,000 and 100,000 instances, with specific labels for dialog acts such as 'inform', 'question', 'directive', and 'commissive', as well as emotion labels like 'anger', 'disgust', 'fear', 'happiness', 'sadness', and 'surprise'. The dataset is divided into training, validation, and test splits, with 11,118 examples for training and 1,000 examples each for validation and testing. The total size of the dataset is approximately 8.63 MB, with a download size of 4.48 MB. DailyDialog is intended for research in dialog systems, providing a resource for developing and evaluating models that can understand and generate human-like dialogues.
