The GovReport dataset is designed for the summarization of long documents, specifically focusing on generating concise summaries from extensive reports. It is a monolingual dataset in English, categorized under the task categories of summarization and text generation, with a size ranging between 10,000 and 100,000 instances. The dataset is structured into three main splits: train, validation, and test, containing 17,517, 973, and 973 examples respectively. Each entry in the dataset consists of two primary fields: 'report', which is a string containing the body of the report, and 'summary', a string that provides a concise summary of the report. The dataset is compatible with the Transformers library's summarization script, requiring a specific configuration for integration. The total download size of the dataset is approximately 506 MB, with an overall dataset size of around 1.06 GB. This dataset is particularly useful for training and evaluating models in the domain of conditional text generation, where the goal is to produce accurate and coherent summaries from lengthy documents. It is adapted from existing research and repositories, providing a valuable resource for developing efficient attention mechanisms for long document summarization.
