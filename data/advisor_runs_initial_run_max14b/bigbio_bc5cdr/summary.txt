The BC5CDR dataset is a comprehensive resource designed for the extraction of chemical-disease relations from scientific literature. It consists of a large annotated text corpus derived from 1,500 PubMed articles, focusing on human annotations of chemicals, diseases, and their interactions. The dataset is monolingual, available in English, and is publicly accessible under the Public Domain Mark 1.0 license. It is specifically tailored for tasks such as Named Entity Recognition (NER), Named Entity Disambiguation (NED), and Relation Extraction (RE), making it a valuable tool for researchers in the biomedical and bioinformatics fields. The dataset is part of the BioCreative V challenge, which aims to advance the development of text mining tools for biomedical research. The BC5CDR dataset is hosted on the BioCreative website, providing a reliable source for those interested in exploring chemical-disease interactions. Its structured format and comprehensive annotations make it suitable for training machine learning models and developing algorithms for automated information extraction in the biomedical domain.
