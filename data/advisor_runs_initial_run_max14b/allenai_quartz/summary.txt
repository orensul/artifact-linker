The QuaRTz dataset is a crowdsourced collection of 3864 multiple-choice questions focused on open-domain qualitative relationships. It is designed for tasks related to question-answering, specifically extractive and open-domain QA. The dataset is monolingual, with all content in English, and is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). The dataset is divided into three splits: training with 2696 examples, validation with 384 examples, and testing with 784 examples. Each question in the dataset is associated with one of 405 unique background sentences or short paragraphs, which provide context for the questions. The dataset includes various features such as question IDs, the questions themselves, multiple-choice options with labels, the correct answer key, and detailed annotations for both the questions and the background paragraphs. These annotations include information on causal and effect properties and directions. The dataset is intended for use in developing and evaluating models that can understand and reason about qualitative relationships in an open-domain context. The total size of the dataset is approximately 1.72 MB, with a download size of 0.49 MB.
