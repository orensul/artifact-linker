The COVID-QA dataset is a specialized question-answering resource designed to facilitate research and development in the domain of COVID-19. It comprises 2,019 question-answer pairs, meticulously annotated by a team of 15 volunteer biomedical experts. These annotations are based on 147 scientific articles from the CORD-19 dataset, ensuring a high level of expertise and accuracy. The dataset is structured in a SQuAD-style format, where each entry includes a question, a context passage from the CORD-19 dataset, and a set of answers with their respective start indices. The dataset is monolingual, with all content in English, and is categorized under the closed-domain and extractive question-answering tasks. It is available under the Apache 2.0 license, making it accessible for a wide range of applications. The primary use cases for COVID-QA include building question-answering models that can assist clinical and scientific researchers, public health authorities, and frontline workers by providing relevant answers to common questions derived from scientific literature. The dataset is intended to support the development of systems that can efficiently locate and present information from research papers, thereby aiding in the understanding and management of COVID-19-related information.
