The Calc-asdiv_a dataset is a collection of simple arithmetic word problems derived from the ASDiv dataset, specifically focusing on arithmetic tasks. It includes a unique 'chain' column, which represents the solution process in a simple HTML-like language, facilitating parsing by tools like BeautifulSoup. This dataset is designed to train Chain-of-Thought reasoning models that can utilize external tools, such as a sympy-based calculator, to enhance the accuracy of their responses. The dataset comprises several attributes: 'id' for example identification, 'question' for the problem description, 'chain' for the series of operations leading to the solution, 'result' for the solution as a string, 'result_float' for the solution as a float, 'result_unit' for the units of the result, 'grade' for the estimated school grade level, and 'source_question' for the origin of the example. The dataset is intended as a testing benchmark without predefined data splits. It is part of a broader initiative to develop models, known as Calcformers, capable of performing calculations during inference. The dataset is available under the CC BY-NC 4.0 license, consistent with its original source, and is part of the Calc-X collection aimed at training such models.
