The ChemSum dataset is a comprehensive collection of academic chemistry papers designed for summarization tasks. It comprises articles from various open-access chemistry journals, including Beilstein, Chem Cell, ChemRxiv, Chemistry Open, Nature Communications Chemistry, PubMed Author Manuscript, PubMed Open Access, Royal Society of Chemistry, and Scientific Reports - Nature. The dataset contains between 100,000 and 1 million entries, with specific counts for each journal source. Each entry in the dataset includes a unique identifier, the article's title, the source journal, the abstract, full-text sections with section boundaries, corresponding section headers, and token counts for both the sections and the abstract. The dataset is structured to facilitate the generation of abstracts from full-text sections, making it suitable for training and evaluating summarization models in the chemistry domain. The data is split into training, validation, and test sets, with 115,956 entries for training, 1,000 for validation, and 2,000 for testing. The dataset is primarily in English and is intended for use in developing and fine-tuning models for scientific summarization, particularly in the fields of chemistry, biology, and medicine.
