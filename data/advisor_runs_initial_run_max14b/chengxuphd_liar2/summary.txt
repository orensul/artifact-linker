The LIAR2 dataset is an enhanced version of the original LIAR dataset, designed for fake news detection tasks. It contains approximately 23,000 entries that have been manually labeled by professional fact-checkers. The dataset is structured to support text classification tasks and is available in English. It is categorized under the size range of 10,000 to 100,000 entries. The dataset is split into training, validation, and test sets with a ratio of 8:1:1, respectively. This structure allows researchers to effectively train, validate, and test their models for fake news detection. The dataset can be accessed through platforms like Huggingface and GitHub, making it easily available for researchers and developers. The intended use cases for the LIAR2 dataset include developing and benchmarking fake news detection systems, particularly those utilizing deep learning and natural language processing techniques. The dataset serves as a valuable resource for advancing research in the domain of misinformation detection and provides a standardized benchmark for evaluating the performance of various classification algorithms in this field.
