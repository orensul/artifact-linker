The dataset 'wine_reviews' contains detailed information about various wines, including their country of origin, description, points, price, province, and variety. The dataset is structured with several features: 'country' and 'province' are strings indicating the geographical origin of the wine, 'description' is a string providing a detailed narrative about the wine, 'points' is an integer representing the wine's rating, and 'price' is a float indicating the cost of the wine. The 'variety' feature is a class label with 30 different wine types, such as Bordeaux-style Red Blend, Chardonnay, and Pinot Noir, among others. The dataset is divided into three splits: training, validation, and test, with 71,504, 12,619, and 21,031 examples respectively. The total dataset size is approximately 30.9 MB, with a download size of around 16.2 MB. This dataset is particularly useful for applications in the wine industry, such as recommendation systems, market analysis, and consumer preference studies, as well as for machine learning tasks involving classification and regression models related to wine characteristics and pricing.
