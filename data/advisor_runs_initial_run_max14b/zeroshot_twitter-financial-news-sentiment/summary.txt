The Twitter Financial News dataset is a monolingual English dataset designed for sentiment analysis in the financial domain. It consists of 11,932 annotated tweets related to finance, collected using the Twitter API. The dataset is structured to support multi-class classification tasks, specifically focusing on identifying the sentiment of tweets as 'Bearish', 'Bullish', or 'Neutral'. The dataset is divided into two splits: a training set with 9,938 instances and a validation set with 2,486 instances. This dataset is particularly useful for applications in finance, such as analyzing market sentiment, understanding public opinion on stocks, and aiding quantitative analysis for hedge funds and market analysts. The dataset is categorized under the size range of 10K to 100K instances and is released under the MIT License, allowing for flexible use in various research and commercial applications. It is tagged with relevant keywords such as finance, markets, stocks, and Wall Street, making it a valuable resource for those interested in financial market analysis and sentiment classification tasks.
