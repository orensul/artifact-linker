The Balanced Choice of Plausible Alternatives (Balanced COPA) dataset is designed for training machine learning models in commonsense causal reasoning. It is an extension of the original COPA dataset, aimed at mitigating the influence of superficial cues and spurious correlations that can skew model performance. The dataset is monolingual, focusing on the English language, and is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). It contains mirrored instances that balance token distribution between correct and incorrect answer choices, addressing the issue where certain tokens appear more frequently in correct answers. This adjustment helps in training models that are robust to such superficial cues. The dataset is structured for multiple-choice question-answering tasks, with data fields including 'premise', 'choice1', 'choice2', 'question', 'label', 'id', and 'mirrored'. It is split into validation and test sets, with 1,000 and 500 instances respectively. The dataset is intended for use in developing and evaluating models that require robust commonsense reasoning capabilities, without relying on easily exploitable token-level cues. It is curated by experts and is available for research and development purposes.
