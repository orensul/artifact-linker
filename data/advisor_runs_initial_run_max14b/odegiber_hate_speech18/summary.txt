The dataset, named 'Hate Speech', contains text data extracted from Stormfront, a white supremacist forum. It is designed for text classification tasks, specifically intent classification, and is monolingual in English. The dataset comprises between 10,000 and 100,000 instances, with each instance including features such as 'text', 'user_id', 'subforum_id', 'num_contexts', and 'label'. The 'label' feature categorizes the text into four classes: 'noHate', 'hate', 'idk/skip', and 'relation'. The dataset is structured to allow the reconstruction of conversations by providing 'user_id' and 'subforum_id'. The 'num_contexts' feature indicates the number of previous posts the annotator reviewed before labeling. The dataset is available under the CC BY-SA 3.0 license and is intended for research and development in detecting hate speech in online forums. It is particularly useful for developing models that can identify and classify hate speech, contributing to the broader effort of understanding and mitigating harmful online content. The dataset is available for download, with a total size of approximately 3.66 MB, and the training split contains 10,944 examples.
