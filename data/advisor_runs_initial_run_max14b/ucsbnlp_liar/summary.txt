The LIAR dataset is designed for the task of fake news detection and contains over 12,800 human-labeled short statements sourced from politifact.com's API. Each statement is evaluated by a politifact.com editor for its truthfulness, with labels ranging from 'false' to 'pants-fire'. The dataset is monolingual, focusing on English, and is categorized under text classification tasks. It includes various features such as the statement ID, label, statement text, subject, speaker, job title, state information, party affiliation, and counts of different truthfulness levels. The dataset is divided into three splits: training, testing, and validation, with 10,269, 1,283, and 1,284 examples respectively. The total dataset size is approximately 3.4 MB, with a download size of about 1 MB. The dataset is intended for use in developing and evaluating models for multi-class classification tasks, specifically in the domain of fake news detection. It provides a balanced distribution of labels, except for the 'pants-fire' category, which has fewer instances. The dataset does not specify a license, and its annotations are expert-generated.
