The dataset comprises approximately 200,000 grade school math word problems, curated by Microsoft and designed to enhance the mathematical capabilities of language models. Each entry in the dataset consists of a question and its corresponding answer, both in English. The answers are generated using Azure GPT4-Turbo, providing a robust foundation for language models to excel in mathematical problem-solving. The dataset is available under the MIT license and is intended for use in the domain of question-answering, specifically focusing on math-related queries. It is not suitable for use in educational systems or organizations. The dataset is structured into a single training split containing 200,035 examples, with a total size of approximately 225 MB. The data is stored in a format that includes two main fields: 'question' and 'answer', both of which are strings. The dataset can be accessed through the Hugging Face repository and is accompanied by a detailed paper that outlines its construction and intended use cases. The dataset does not contain any personal or sensitive information, and it is specifically tailored for enhancing the performance of language models in solving math problems.
