The Asclepius dataset is a synthetic clinical notes and instruction dataset designed for building clinical large language models (LLMs). It is available under the CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0 license and is intended for tasks such as question-answering, summarization, and text generation within the medical domain. The dataset is composed of Clinical Note - Question - Answer pairs, synthesized from PMC-Patients case reports using GPT-3.5. It includes 157,000 synthetic discharge summaries, making it a substantial resource with a size category between 100,000 and 1 million entries. The dataset supports eight tasks: Named Entity Recognition, Abbreviation Expansion, Relation Extraction, Temporal Information Extraction, Coreference Resolution, Paraphrasing, Summarization, and Question Answering. The data is structured in a CSV format with fields such as patient_id, patient, question, answer, and task. The dataset is primarily in English and is designed to facilitate the development of clinical LLMs by providing a rich set of synthetic clinical scenarios. It is hosted on GitHub and further details, including prompts used for data generation, can be accessed there. The dataset is also linked to several models available on Hugging Face, such as Asclepius-7B and Asclepius-13B.
