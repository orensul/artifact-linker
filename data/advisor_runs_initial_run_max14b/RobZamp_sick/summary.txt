The SICK (Sentences Involving Compositional Knowledge) dataset is a benchmark designed for research in compositional distributional semantic models (CDSMs). It contains approximately 10,000 English sentence pairs, focusing on lexical, syntactic, and semantic phenomena relevant to CDSMs. The dataset is monolingual and crowdsourced, with annotations for two primary semantic tasks: relatedness in meaning, rated on a 5-point scale, and entailment relations, categorized as entailment, contradiction, or neutral. The dataset is divided into three splits: training (4,439 examples), validation (495 examples), and test (4,906 examples), with a total size of approximately 2.6 MB. Each data instance includes fields such as sentence pairs, entailment labels, relatedness scores, and original sentences from which the pairs were derived. The sentences are sourced from extended datasets like image-flickr-8k and semeval2012-sts-msr-video. The SICK dataset is licensed under cc-by-nc-sa-3.0 and is intended for use in text classification tasks, specifically natural language inference. It was notably used in SemEval-2014 Task 1 and is freely available for research purposes, providing a valuable resource for the development and evaluation of CDSMs.
