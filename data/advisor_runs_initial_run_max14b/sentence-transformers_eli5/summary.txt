The ELI5 dataset is a collection of question-answer pairs sourced from the Explain Like I'm 5 subreddit. It is designed for use in feature-extraction and sentence-similarity tasks, particularly with Sentence Transformers to train embedding models. The dataset is monolingual, containing English language data, and falls within the size category of 100K to 1M examples. The primary subset, named 'pair', includes two main columns: 'question' and 'answer', both of which are strings. The dataset is structured to facilitate the training of models that require understanding and generating human-like responses to questions. It is particularly useful for applications in natural language processing where understanding the context and providing coherent answers is crucial. The dataset is available in a training split with 325,475 examples, occupying approximately 173 MB of storage. It is accessible for download with a size of around 113 MB. This dataset is not deduplicated, meaning it retains all original entries from the source. It serves as a valuable resource for developing models that can comprehend and generate explanations in a simplified manner, akin to explaining complex topics to a five-year-old.
