The Bitext Customer Service Tagged Training Dataset is a hybrid synthetic dataset designed for fine-tuning Large Language Models (LLMs) such as GPT, Mistral, and OpenELM. It is specifically tailored for the customer support sector, facilitating domain adaptation through a two-step fine-tuning process. The dataset is intended for use in intent detection within customer service, featuring 27 intents across 10 categories, with a total of 26,872 question/answer pairs. Each intent is supported by approximately 1,000 examples, and the dataset includes 30 entity/slot types and 12 language generation tags. The dataset covers a wide range of verticals, including automotive, retail banking, healthcare, and telecommunications, among others. Each entry in the dataset contains fields such as 'flags', 'instruction', 'category', 'intent', and 'response'. The dataset is rich in linguistic phenomena, offering tags for lexical, syntactic, and stylistic variations, which allow for customization to different user profiles and language registers. This makes it suitable for developing robust virtual assistants capable of handling diverse language use cases in customer service scenarios.
