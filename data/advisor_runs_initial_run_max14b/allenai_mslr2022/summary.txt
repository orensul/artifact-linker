The MSLR2022 dataset is designed for the Multidocument Summarization for Literature Review Shared Task, focusing on summarizing medical evidence from various clinical studies into literature reviews. This dataset is crucial for advancing natural language processing (NLP) techniques in the medical domain, particularly for automating the synthesis of clinical evidence, which is traditionally a labor-intensive process. The dataset is monolingual, containing English texts, and falls within the size category of 10,000 to 100,000 instances. It is sourced from extended datasets like MS^2 and Cochrane, and is licensed under Apache 2.0, allowing for broad use and distribution. The dataset is structured to support tasks in summarization and text-to-text generation, providing a rich resource for developing and evaluating models that can interpret and summarize complex medical information. The annotations are expert-generated, ensuring high-quality data for training and testing NLP models. The intended use cases include improving model interpretability, developing scaffolding tasks, and enhancing automated evaluation methods in the domain of medical literature review summarization.
