The FiNER-Open Research Dataset (FiNER-ORD) is a manually annotated dataset of financial news articles in English, sourced from webz.io. It comprises 47,851 news articles, each formatted as a JSON document containing metadata such as the source, publication date, author, and title. For the purpose of named entity recognition (NER) in financial news, 220 documents were randomly sampled and manually annotated, resulting in 201 usable articles after filtering out empty ones. The dataset is annotated using Doccano, an open-source tool, to label entities such as person (PER), location (LOC), and organization (ORG). The dataset is split into training, validation, and test sets, with annotations performed by different annotators to ensure unbiased results. The dataset is structured in a CoNLL 2003 format, with fields including document ID, sentence ID, token, and classification labels. It is available in IOB format and is intended for use in token classification tasks, specifically named entity recognition in the financial domain. The dataset is monolingual, focusing solely on English, and is licensed under cc-by-nc-4.0, making it suitable for non-commercial research and educational purposes.
