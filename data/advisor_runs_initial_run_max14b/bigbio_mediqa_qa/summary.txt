The MEDIQA QA dataset is part of the ACL-BioNLP 2019 shared task, designed to foster research in Natural Language Inference (NLI), Recognizing Question Entailment (RQE), and their applications in medical Question Answering (QA). The dataset is monolingual, focusing on the English language, and is publicly accessible, although its licensing details are unspecified. The primary task associated with this dataset is question answering, where participants are required to filter and classify provided answers as correct or incorrect, and subsequently re-rank them. The dataset does not include PubMed data, indicating that it may not directly incorporate biomedical literature from that source. The MEDIQA QA dataset is intended for use in developing and evaluating systems that can effectively handle medical question answering tasks, making it a valuable resource for researchers and developers working in the field of biomedical natural language processing. The dataset's homepage provides further information and access, and a mailing list is available for community discussions and updates related to the MEDIQA challenge.
