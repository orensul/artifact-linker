The 'Measuring Hate Speech' dataset is a comprehensive collection of 39,565 comments annotated by 7,912 annotators, resulting in 135,556 combined rows. It is designed for tasks such as hate speech detection, sentiment classification, and multi-label classification. The dataset is monolingual, focusing on English, and is licensed under cc-by-4.0. It includes a variety of features, with the primary outcome being the 'hate speech score,' a continuous measure indicating the level of hatefulness in a comment. The dataset also provides 10 constituent ordinal labels, such as sentiment, respect, insult, and violence, which contribute to the overall hate speech score. Additionally, it includes information on 8 target identity groups and 42 subgroups, covering aspects like race, religion, gender, and political ideology. Annotator demographics are also detailed, with 6 main categories and 40 subgroups. The dataset is intended for research in text classification, particularly in understanding and measuring hate speech, and it incorporates an IRT adjustment to account for variations in annotator interpretations. The dataset is available for download and can be accessed using Python code provided in the documentation.
