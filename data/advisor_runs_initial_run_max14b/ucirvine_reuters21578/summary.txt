The Reuters-21578 Text Categorization Collection is a widely utilized dataset for research in text categorization. It comprises news articles from the Reuters financial newswire service, collected in 1987. The dataset is structured into three configurations: ModApte, ModHayes, and ModLewis, each with distinct data splits for training, testing, and unused data. The dataset includes features such as text, topics, lewis_split, cgis_split, old_id, new_id, places, people, orgs, exchanges, date, and title. The ModApte configuration contains 9603 training examples, 3299 test examples, and 722 unused examples, with a total dataset size of approximately 13 MB. ModHayes has 20856 training examples and 722 test examples, with a dataset size of around 20 MB. ModLewis includes 13625 training examples, 6188 test examples, and 722 unused examples, with a dataset size of about 19 MB. The dataset is intended for research purposes only, with a focus on developing and evaluating text categorization models.
