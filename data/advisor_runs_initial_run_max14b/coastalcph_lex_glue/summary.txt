The LexGLUE dataset is a comprehensive collection designed for legal text processing tasks, specifically in the domain of question-answering and text classification. It is monolingual, focusing on English, and is licensed under the CC-BY-4.0 license. The dataset is categorized into several configurations, each tailored for specific legal tasks, including case_hold, ecthr_a, ecthr_b, eurlex, ledgar, scotus, and unfair_tos. These configurations cover a range of legal documents and annotations, such as court cases, legal agreements, and terms of service. The dataset size varies across configurations, with the total number of examples ranging from 10,000 to 100,000. Each configuration includes features like text and labels, with labels being either single or multi-class. The dataset is split into training, validation, and test sets, with varying numbers of examples and byte sizes. LexGLUE is intended for use in developing and evaluating models for legal text classification and question-answering tasks, providing a robust benchmark for legal natural language processing applications. The dataset is available for download from its GitHub repository, offering a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners in the legal tech domain.
