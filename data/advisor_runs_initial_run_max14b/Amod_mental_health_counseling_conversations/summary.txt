The Amod - Mental Health Counseling Conversations dataset is a collection of real one-on-one mental health counseling dialogues between individuals and licensed professionals. It is designed to support tasks such as text generation and question-answering, particularly in the context of providing empathetic and contextually aware responses. The dataset is composed of question-answer pairs, where each instance includes a 'Context' field representing the user's question and a 'Response' field containing the psychologist's answer. This dataset is available in English and falls within the size category of 1,000 to 10,000 entries. It is intended for use in training and evaluating language models that aim to deliver safe and context-aware mental health advice. The data is sourced from two public mental health platforms, ensuring that all personally identifiable information has been removed to maintain privacy. The dataset is released under the RAIL-D license, which allows free non-commercial research use, while commercial use requires a donation to a mental health helpline. Redistribution is permitted only in its original form, and content modification is restricted to filtering or subsetting without altering individual question-answer pairs.
