FiNER-139 is a comprehensive dataset consisting of 1.1 million sentences annotated with eXtensive Business Reporting Language (XBRL) tags. These sentences are extracted from annual and quarterly reports of publicly traded companies in the United States. The dataset is designed for tasks such as named-entity recognition and entity extraction, focusing on a large set of 139 entity types, which is significantly broader than typical entity extraction tasks. FiNER-139 emphasizes numeric tokens, where the context determines the correct tag rather than the token itself. The dataset is monolingual, containing English language reports, and is categorized under the size range of 1M to 10M instances. It is intended to facilitate research in the financial domain, particularly in automating the tagging of financial reports with XBRL tags, which is a labor-intensive process when done manually. The dataset is curated from approximately 10,000 reports filed with the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and spans a five-year period from 2016 to 2020. It is licensed under cc-by-sa-4.0, allowing for broad use in research and development of financial natural language processing applications.
