The SAMSum Corpus is a dataset designed for the task of summarization, specifically focusing on messenger-like conversations. It contains approximately 16,369 conversations, each paired with a human-written summary. These conversations were crafted by linguists fluent in English, who were instructed to create dialogues that mirror their real-life messaging interactions. The dataset captures a variety of conversational styles, including informal, semi-formal, and formal exchanges, and may include slang, emoticons, and typos. The dataset is monolingual, with all content in English, and is categorized under the size range of 10K to 100K instances. The data is split into training, validation, and test sets, with 14,732, 818, and 819 conversations respectively. Each conversation includes a unique identifier, the dialogue text, and its corresponding summary. The SAMSum Corpus was developed by Samsung R&D Institute Poland and is available under a non-commercial license (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0). It is intended for research purposes, providing a resource for developing and evaluating summarization models that can handle conversational data typical of messaging applications.
