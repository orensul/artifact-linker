The ChemProt dataset is a specialized resource designed for the identification and extraction of chemical-protein interactions. It is part of the BioCreative VI challenge and focuses on the domain of biomedical text mining. The dataset is monolingual, containing English language data, and is publicly available under the Public Domain Mark 1.0 license. It is structured to support tasks such as relation extraction and named entity recognition, making it suitable for researchers and developers working on natural language processing applications in the biomedical field. The dataset is divided into several configurations, including 'chemprot_bigbio_kb', 'chemprot_full_source', and 'chemprot_shared_task_eval_source', each with specific features and data splits for training, testing, and validation. The dataset includes detailed annotations of entities such as chemicals and proteins, along with their interactions, which are categorized as agonists or antagonists. The dataset is substantial in size, with the 'chemprot_bigbio_kb' configuration alone comprising over 8.6 million bytes and 2,482 examples across its splits. This dataset is intended for use in developing and evaluating models that can accurately identify and classify chemical-protein interactions in scientific literature.
